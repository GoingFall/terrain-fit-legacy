Mod: directional valley and snow mask, both driven by the runtime config

- valleyAngle / valleyDepth / valleyWidth / valleyMeander: rotate into (u, v) and carve a wandering
  band; the Python mirror has the same code. Verified: turning it on drops the local minimum from
  123 to 110 blocks while leaving the mean height alone (a carved band, not a global shift).
- snowMaskFreq / snowMaskThreshold: a low-frequency noise decides which regions read as snowy,
  independent of altitude, because the reference's 63.9% snow cannot come from its own hypsometry
  (~10% of its area is above y=193; the snow is biome-driven).
- both are in FitParams and tools/fit_model.py, so a config edit changes them without a rebuild.

First run with both: distance 0.646, anisotropy 320 (target 85.6 -- the valley term can exceed the
target, so it is tunable in the right direction), snow 97.5% (threshold too permissive).

Residual breakdown at 0.676 shows what is left: water 0% vs 0.59 (the sea level sits below the
terrain), spectrum 128-256 at 5.3% vs 37.7% (the model has no mid-scale band), gt4 0.83% vs 0.14%
(too many small cliffs), ridge density 957 vs 278 (crests too close together), anisotropy 320 vs 86.
