Skeleton model ported to the mod, and measured in game

FitParams now carries a massif list (oriented gaussians) and optional valley polylines; FitTerrain
switches to the skeleton whenever that list is non-empty, so a config edit changes the whole terrain
generator with no rebuild.

In-game measurement of the skeleton (11 massifs fitted to region r.0.0):

    relief 209 (target 238)     slope p50 0.71 (exact)    p99 2.50 (exact)
    ridge density 22 vs 278     water 3.7% vs 0.59%       snow 28.2% vs 63.9%
    distance 0.513  -- the noise model managed 0.62-0.68

Two findings from this run:
- switching the test biome from snowy_plains to plains cut snow from 85% to 28% without touching our
  own rule, which confirms the biome's own snow layers were stacking on top of ours
- ridge density collapsed from 291 to 22 at the same time: the earlier 291 was merely the 1-block
  snow-layer bumps being counted as crests. The skeleton is genuinely too smooth, which is also why
  the 64-128 Hz band is empty.

coverage to the reference's 64%.
