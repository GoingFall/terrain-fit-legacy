Skeleton model: placed landforms instead of stationary noise

tools/skeleton_model.py replaces the noise sum with a handful of oriented gaussian massifs (plus
valley polylines), seeded at the reference's own peaks and refined by coordinate descent against the
reference height field.

    noise model      rmse 38.1 blocks   correlation 0.64
    skeleton model   rmse  9.1 blocks   correlation 0.98

preview/skeleton_compare.png shows why: the massifs reproduce the reference's large slopes and the
valley between them, which the noise model could never place.

preview/skeleton_quantised.png answers the terracing question: quantising the smooth skeleton to
whole blocks reproduces the reference's contour-like 1-block steps, so the author's fine terraces are
not a missing feature but a consequence of quantising a smooth slope.
