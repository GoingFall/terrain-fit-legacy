Two metric bugs that invalidated the snow tuning

1. cover.*_pct divided by the whole grid instead of the valid columns. A mod world with 25600 valid
   columns out of a 1024x1024 grid reported 37% snow where it actually had 95%.

2. snow cover counted only "a snow layer above the ground" (s_raw > g_raw). A generated world paints
   snow as the surface block, so the two sides were measuring different things. The surface block is
   now included.

With both fixed: reference snow 63.9%, mod 95.3% at snowLine 170 and 97% at 175 -- i.e. altitude is
not what drives the reference's snow at all. Its own hypsometry puts only ~10% of the area above
y=193, so the 63.9% must come from the snowy_plains biome's surface rule across ~50% of the map.
Matching it needs a snow *mask* (low-frequency noise), not a height threshold.

Also from this round: the mod loop runs one iteration in ~80 s (config write, server boot, forceload,
measure) and currently reaches distance 0.62-0.65 against r.0.0.
