Multi-biome dimensions need a preset in 1.20.1, not an inline list

Log said: No key preset in MapLike[{type: multi_noise, biomes: [...]}]. The inline biome list form
is not accepted by the 1.20.1 codec, so a multi-biome dimension needs a
data/<ns>/worldgen/multi_noise_biome_source_parameter_list/<name>.json registry entry, referenced
as {"type":"minecraft:multi_noise","preset":"<ns>:<name>"}.

- the exporter emits that parameter list when more than one biome is requested, and keeps a
  minecraft:fixed source for a single biome
